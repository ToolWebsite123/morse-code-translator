## 1. Executive Summary: Topical Authority & Pedagogical Integrity

The **Morse Code Platform** was evaluated as a comprehensive online educational institution and utility reference. 

Unlike legacy competitor websites that treat Morse code merely as a mechanical string-replacement trick, this platform grounds its content in **international telecommunication standards (Recommendation ITU-R M.1677-1)**. The curriculum embodies modern cognitive learning principles, guiding students through a structured pedagogical funnel:

$$\textbf{Discover} \longrightarrow \textbf{Understand} \longrightarrow \textbf{Learn} \longrightarrow \textbf{Practice} \longrightarrow \textbf{Test} \longrightarrow \textbf{Improve}$$

## 3. Topical Authority Deficiencies & Content Evolution Plan

To establish unassailable domain authority and capture high-intent organic search queries, the platform content should evolve through four distinct editorial actions:

### 3.1 What to Create New (High-ROI Pillars)
1. **`/morse-code-sos` (Emergency Authority):** Detailed guide on the SOS prosign, RMS Titanic distress history, maritime survival protocols, and optical signaling.
2. **`/morse-code-sheet` (Classroom & Field Utility):** High-contrast printable vector PDF cheat sheet.
3. **`/morse-code-numbers` (Numeric Guide):** Comprehensive analysis of 5-element numbers and contest cut numbers.
4. **`/morse-code-q-codes` (Amateur Radio Community):** Top 25 ham radio Q-codes and CW conversational abbreviations.
5. **`/history-of-morse-code` (Academic & Educational):** The historical evolution of telegraphy from Samuel Morse to ITU-R standards.

### 3.2 What to Expand (Existing Pages)
* **`/reference`:** Expand the character table with direct audio recording sample variations (e.g. 550 Hz vs 750 Hz pitch comparisons).
* **`/practice`:** Introduce "Word Drills" (common English 3-letter words like THE, AND, FOR) to bridge single letters and conversational text.

### 3.3 What to Avoid (Negative Value)
* **Avoid Thin Programmatic Pages:** Do NOT create 26 separate thin pages for individual letters (e.g. `/morse-code-for-a`, `/morse-code-for-b`). Search engines classify single-character pages as low-quality doorway pages. Keep all characters organized within `/reference` and `/learn`.
